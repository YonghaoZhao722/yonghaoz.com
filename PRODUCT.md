# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Prospective advisors, research collaborators, academic peers, and recruiters who want to quickly understand Yonghao Zhao's background, research interests, publications, and experience.

## Product Purpose

Present a concise, credible academic profile that makes research identity, training, current work, and publication evidence easy to scan and verify.

## Brand Personality

Scholarly, restrained, and clear. The site should feel personal and confident without becoming decorative or promotional.

## Anti-references

Avoid flashy portfolio conventions, excessive cards, gradients, decorative animation, dense dashboards, and styling that competes with the academic content.

## Design Principles

- Put research identity and evidence first.
- Preserve a calm editorial rhythm and clear hierarchy.
- Keep interactions and navigation direct and unsurprising.
- Make changes surgical so established visual continuity is preserved.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain readable contrast, responsive layouts, semantic structure, keyboard-accessible links, and legible typography without imposing an additional formal WCAG target.
